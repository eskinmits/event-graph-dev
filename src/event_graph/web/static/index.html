              How this is computed

              A random walk starts at the event and, at every step, either moves to a random neighbour or jumps back to the event (15%). After four steps, the share of time it spent on each artist is that artist's score. Genre and city nodes are never walked through: they connect everything, and neither are duplicate listings of the event (same_as), which still carry its artist and would give the answer away. Nothing here reads a name or a title. The paths are the routes that carried most of the walk to each artist; hover a row to trace them.
